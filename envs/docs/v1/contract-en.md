SYSTEM MAINTENANCE SERVICES AGREEMENT No. 07/2026

concluded on 2 January 2026 between Kwadrat Sp. z o.o. (the Customer) and Chmurex S.A. (the Provider).

§1. General provisions
1. This Agreement sets out the terms on which the Provider maintains and develops the "Kwadrat Order" order management system for the Customer.
2. The annexes listed in §14 form an integral part of the Agreement. In case of conflict the Agreement prevails unless a signed amendment provides otherwise.
3. The Parties shall cooperate in good faith to perform the Agreement properly.

§2. Definitions
1. Business Day means any day from Monday to Friday excluding public holidays in Poland.
2. Ticket means information about an Incident submitted by the Customer through the service portal or by phone.
3. Incident means any event causing an interruption or degradation of the System.
4. Maintenance Window means a period agreed in advance in which the Provider performs planned work.
5. Unavailability means a period in which the System does not perform the core functions listed in Annex 1.
6. Monthly Fee means the remuneration referred to in §5(2).

§3. Subject matter
1. The Provider maintains the infrastructure, monitors it, handles Tickets and applies security updates.
2. Development of the System is ordered separately and priced according to Annex 3.
3. The Provider keeps technical documentation and makes it available to the Customer on request.

§4. Obligations of the Parties
1. The Customer shall give the Provider access to test environments to the extent necessary.
2. The Provider shall appoint a service manager responsible for contact with the Customer.
3. The Parties shall hold monthly service quality reviews and record them in a note.

§5. Remuneration
1. Fees for development work are based on the rates in Annex 3.
2. The Monthly Fee is PLN 48,000 net, payable in arrears within 21 days of receipt of the invoice.

§6. Service levels and contractual penalties
1. The Provider shall ensure System Availability of at least 99.5% in each calendar month.
2. Unavailability is counted in full minutes, from the moment it starts until the core functions are restored.
3. The permitted Unavailability in a month (the Allowance) is 0.5% of the number of minutes in that month.
4. For each started hour of Unavailability exceeding the Allowance the Provider shall pay a contractual penalty of 2% of the Monthly Fee.
5. Unavailability does not include: (a) work in a Maintenance Window announced to the Customer at least 72 hours in advance, provided a single window does not exceed 4 hours; (b) interruptions caused by force majeure confirmed in writing by both Parties.
6. Total contractual penalties for a month may not exceed 20% of the Monthly Fee.
7. Contractual penalties for a month are reduced by any compensation (service credits) granted by the Provider for the same month.
